      Global context
      Worldwide, employment has been shifting away from the sectors most exposed to automation: Services' share of global employment rose from 44.6% to 50.6% (2010–2025), while Agriculture fell from 32.6% to 25.8% over the same period (ILOSTAT).

          2020 dip
